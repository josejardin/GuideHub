Ethical Standards

Confidentiality & Care Commitment

Your trust is our cornerstone. In accordance with the Philippine Guidance and Counseling Act of 2004 (Republic Act No. 9258) and the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) , all disclosures, session notes, and records remain strictly confidential within the guidance office.

Protected Privacy

No information shared during counseling will be disclosed to your professors, department, peers, or family without your explicit written consent.

Mandatory Safety Exceptions

Confidentiality is legally waived only when there is imminent risk of harm to yourself (self-harm), danger to others, or reported abuse of vulnerable individuals.
