Your Trust is Sacred

Your Story Stays Safe with Us

Opening up takes courage. In full compliance with the Philippine Guidance and Counseling Act of 2004 (RA 9258) and the Data Privacy Act, everything you share remains strictly confidential between you and your counselor—honored with deep respect, privacy, and empathy.

Unconditional Safe Space

Nothing you share will ever be disclosed to your professors, peers, or family without your voluntary permission. You are free to speak your truth.

Looking Out for Your Safety

Because we care deeply about your life and well-being, confidentiality is only waived when there is an immediate, critical danger of self-harm or threat to life.
