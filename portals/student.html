Your Safe Space & Confidentiality Promise

Guided by the Philippine Guidance & Counseling Act (RA 9258)

Your trust is sacred. Here is how we protect your story:

1. A Safe, Confidential Space: Whatever you share stays strictly between you and your Registered Guidance Counselor. No professors, peers, or staff have access to your personal reflections.

2. Keeping You Safe (Mandatory Care): Because your life and well-being are our highest priority, counselors are legally and ethically obligated to step in only if:

  • Imminent danger of self-harm / suicide.
  • Imminent threat of harm toward others.
  • Suspected abuse or neglect of a minor.
  • Court subpoena.
