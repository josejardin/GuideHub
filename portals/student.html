Informed Consent & Confidentiality Agreement

Philippine Guidance & Counseling Act of 2004 (RA 9258)

Confidentiality terms and disclosure boundaries:

1. Confidentiality Commitment: All counseling discussions remain confidential between you and the Registered Guidance Counselor. No faculty or students have access to your clinical records.

2. Mandatory Reporting Exceptions: Counselors are legally required to break confidentiality under specific circumstances:

  • Imminent danger of self-harm / suicide.
  • Imminent threat of harm toward others.
  • Suspected abuse or neglect of a minor.
  • Court subpoena.
